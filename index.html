Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who We Are

Challenge World ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile game developed and operated by Challenge World. By playing, you agree to the terms in this policy.

2. What We Collect

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email address and password (authentication only — password is never stored in plain text)
  • Display name (derived from your email unless you change it)
  • Game progress: levels completed, scores, accuracy, time taken
  • Device identifiers used by RevenueCat for purchase verification
  • Anonymised usage and performance data (via Firebase Analytics)

We do not collect: location, contacts, microphone recordings, or any financial information (payments are handled entirely by Apple / Google).

3. How We Use It

  • To operate the game and save your progress across devices
  • To display your rank on the leaderboard (display name + level only)
  • To process purchases and restore them on new devices
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To improve the game based on aggregated, anonymised usage patterns

4. Who We Share It With

We share data only with the following processors, strictly for operating the game:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, analytics
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to withdraw consent at any time (which requires account deletion). To exercise any of these rights, email us at blueskiiye@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Children

Challenge World is rated for users aged 12 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact us at blueskiiye@gmail.com if you believe your child has created an account.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in recoverable form. Firestore security rules ensure every user can only access their own data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the game or applicable law. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
