Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This is the privacy policy for Challenge World. We have tried to write it in plain language so you can actually understand what it says. If something is unclear, email us at blueskiiye@gmail.com.

1. Who We Are

Challenge World is a mobile game made by an independent developer based in Belgium. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

2. What We Collect and Why

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Your password is hashed before it is stored and we cannot read it in any form. We use the part of your email before the @ symbol as your default display name, which you can change at any time in your profile.

As you play, we store your game progress: which levels you have completed, your scores, your accuracy, and the time you took per level. This is what allows you to pick up where you left off across devices.

Your display name and your highest completed level appear on the global leaderboard. Everything else about your account remains private.

When a crash occurs, Firebase Crashlytics automatically sends us a report containing your device model, operating system version, and a technical stack trace. This does not include your name, email address, or any gameplay data.

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played in aggregate. This gives us anonymous data such as how many people reach a certain level, which challenge types take the longest, and how often sessions are started. No personally identifying information is included in this data.

If you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes the transaction on our behalf. RevenueCat receives a device identifier and your purchase history so that your unlocked content is restored if you reinstall the app or switch devices. We never see your payment card or billing information. All payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google.

We do not collect your location. We do not access your contacts.

Three challenge types in the game use your device microphone: the breath challenge (which measures breath intensity to detect when you blow), the hearing challenge (which measures ambient sound levels), and the voice recall challenge (which uses speech recognition to check your spoken answer). In all three cases, audio is processed entirely on your device in real time. No audio recordings are stored, transmitted to our servers, or shared with any third party.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (EU and UK Users)

We process your account and progress data to perform the contract you entered into when you created an account. Without this data the game cannot function.

We process crash reports and anonymous analytics under our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and improving it over time. This data is either fully anonymous or contains only technical device information.

We process purchase data to fulfil our obligation to deliver the content you paid for.

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We share data only with the service providers we need to run the game:

  • Google Firebase: account authentication, cloud database, crash reporting, and analytics
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification and content restoration
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: payment processing

These providers act as data processors under our instructions. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or any other third parties.

Google's servers are located in the United States. Transfers of data from the EU to Google are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data associated with it is permanently deleted within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated statistics that cannot be linked to any individual may be retained indefinitely to help us understand long-term usage trends.

6. Your Rights

If you are in the EU, the UK, or another region with similar data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your account and all associated data
  • Ask us to restrict how we process your data in certain circumstances
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing that is based on our legitimate interest

To exercise any of these rights, email us at blueskiiye@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Belgium, that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens / Autorite de protection des donnees (APD/GBA) at gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

7. Children

Challenge World is rated for users aged 12 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe your child has created an account, please contact us at blueskiiye@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and cannot be recovered by us or anyone else. Firestore security rules are configured so that each user can only read and write their own data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or to applicable law. If we make significant changes, we will let you know through the app. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact
